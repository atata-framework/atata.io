is released with an upgrade of Selenium.WebDriver to v4.48.0.
{:.lead}

<!--more-->

## Changelog

### Changed

- Upgrade Selenium.WebDriver package reference to v4.48.0
- Upgrade Atata.WebDriverExtras package reference to v3.9.0

## Summary

This is a minor update of Atata with an upgrade of Selenium.WebDriver package reference from v4.45.0 to v4.48.0.
Take a look at [Selenium.WebDriver CHANGELOG](https://github.com/SeleniumHQ/selenium/blob/trunk/dotnet/CHANGELOG)
to find out what was changed there.
